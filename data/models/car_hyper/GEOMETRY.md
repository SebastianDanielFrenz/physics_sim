## Exterior selection sidecar

car_hyper.flow.json uses physics_sim.visual_flow_selection/1 and pins source_glb_sha256. Each ranges entry has node, material, first_triangle and triangle_count, identifying a half-open triangle interval within that node/material glTF primitive's FINAL index array. The current selection contains94,204 triangles. No late degeneracy cleanup is allowed to shift ranges: generator fails explicitly if late cleanup removes any triangle. Consumers must verify hash, bounds and absence of overlap before extraction. Coordinates remain ISO rig-ground origin, not chassis CoM; apply the consumer's existing vehicle alignment rather than a guessed shift.

Selection contains original outer body/glazing skins plus external aero/lamps/wheels/mirrors/exhaust generated from the same source. It excludes reversed inner shell skins/returns, upholstery/headliner/dashboard/seats, frunk tub, engine and engine-bay internals. These are visual candidate surfaces, not a fluid-solid boundary. Outer skin ranges alone can have boundary edges where backing was deliberately excluded. Mechanical rest pose is closed doors/straight wheels/rest wing; configuration-dependent exports must pose the rig explicitly and record provenance.

## Unresolved CFD preparation

cfd_ready stays false even with closed visual topology. The model is a multi-component visual assembly, NOT a boolean union. Known construction overlaps include lower-body/greenhouse belt flanges, shell/cabin lining, body/diffuser and splitter attachment, wing struts/pivots/end plates, tyre/rim/hub/spoke/brake assemblies, grille/lamp overlays and tube/soot interfaces. Offset inner skins at tight arch/panel curvature may intersect locally and have not been certified. Intersection-free geometry, occluded-face removal and clearance over full steering/suspension/door/wing travel have not been certified.

Intakes/grilles are recessed or textured backed visual facades, not resolved cooling ducts. Do not infer cooling drag, radiator losses, exhaust flow or vent mass flow. The exhaust bore terminates at the decorative soot backing; the model is not the engine pipe-network domain. Wheel arches and rim ventilation spaces remain geometrically open. Any CFD union/voxel exterior approximation must explicitly record sealed-panel/backed-grille assumptions, resolution and geometry hash, and is a separate derivative. The source visual GLB should be preserved. Real fluid-solid topology checks, solver setup, convergence and coefficient validation remain required; no CFD coefficients or drag changes are supplied here.

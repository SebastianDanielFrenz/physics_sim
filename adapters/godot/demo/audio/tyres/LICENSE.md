# Asphalt squeal sample

Author: audible-edge (Tom Haigh). Loop edited by qubodup.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/car-tire-squeal-skid-loop
License: CC BY 3.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Original: tires_squal_loop.wav, 96 kHz mono 24-bit, three seconds.
Changes: resampled to 24 kHz, converted to 16-bit PCM, peak normalized to 0.8.
Playback crossfades the loop boundary. This is a generic tyre effect, not a measured hypercar recording.

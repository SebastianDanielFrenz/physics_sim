Drag a point on the active curve (pick it above, or click its line) — it moves in both rpm and torque, clamped strictly between its neighbours and to that curve's own rules (motoring stays ≤ 0; the motoring curve's rpm-0 first point stays pinned). Snaps to 50 rpm / 1 N·m; hold Shift for free movement. Click an empty spot on or near the active curve to insert a point there; double-click or right-click a point to delete it. Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y (or Ctrl+Shift+Z) undo/redo.
